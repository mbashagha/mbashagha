How I work

🔍

Start with research

Good design is an argument backed by evidence. I talk to people, dig into the problem, and bring the reasoning along with the pixels.

🛠️

Build with engineers

The best work happens shoulder-to-shoulder with the people shipping it. I prototype, I code enough to be dangerous, and I stay through execution.

Measure what ships

Launch isn't the finish line. I follow the data after release to learn whether the design actually worked — and iterate when it didn't.
